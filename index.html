Amsterdam · a nanny

Come, let’s go and look at something.

That is what I say to a child when we have a day together. Then we go. To a painting, to the ducks under the bridge, to your kitchen to bake. I am Wietske. I look after children in Amsterdam for an afternoon, a day or a night, the way I would want someone to look after my own.

Why

I love children.

I have always loved children. Their honesty, their questions, the way they are completely in the moment. Being with them is the most natural thing I know.

I believe every child is made in the image of God and is a wonder. I want to help them grow up good: patient, honest, kind, brave, grateful. Not by lecturing, but by being with them, by what we look at together, by how we treat the ducks and the museum guard and each other. Virtue is caught more than taught, and a day is long enough to catch something.

That is why I do this. It comes from my Catholic faith, and you are welcome whatever you believe. All I ask is that you want your child loved well for a day.

What I am not: a nursery or registered childcare, so there is no childcare allowance. I am one person, with a certificate of conduct, first aid for children, and insurance.

How a day with me goes

Six small things, every time.

  1. We say hello. I kneel down and ask your child’s name. I show what is in my bag: a sketchbook, pencils, and a question for the day. What is the oldest thing we will see today?
  2. We go. At your child’s pace. My phone stays in my bag.
  3. We eat. Something real, organic when I can.
  4. We rest. A bench, a story, a drawing of what we saw.
  5. One beautiful thing. Before we go home I ask what was most beautiful today. I write it down in your child’s words.
  6. I bring your child home. With the card, and a few photographs of the day. Your child’s face stays yours; I never put it online.

Three kinds of day

An afternoon, a day, or a night.

I keep it simple: one price per kind of day, tickets included, for one or two children from the same family. A third child is fine if we talk first. The prices are without 21% VAT; I will always tell you the full amount before you decide anything.

Who will be there

Me. Later, perhaps, a few people I trust.

For now, if you book a day, it is me who comes. If Wonder Nanny grows, it will be with people who have spent a day beside me first, who have a certificate of conduct and first aid for children, and who make the same promise:

I am here, fully. I am patient. I take a child only where I would take my own. I tell the parents the truth about the day. What I see in a home stays there.

If that is you too, I would like to meet you. Wonder Nanny grows out of Catholic faith and is open to anyone who lives like this. Write to me.

The gift

A tenth of every day goes to children who have no one.

I help build a home for orphaned children in Bugiri, Uganda: Family of Pure Grace. Ten percent of every day you book goes there. You will see it on your invoice. Every few months I will show what has been sent. And for every ten days I am booked, I give one afternoon to a mother in my parish who is raising her children alone. I do not work for money. I work to give.

Things you may be wondering

Plainly.

Is this registered childcare?

No. I am a private nanny, not a gastouderbureau or kinderopvang in the national register, so you cannot claim childcare allowance. I have a certificate of conduct (VOG), first aid for children, and insurance for looking after them.

What happens with the photographs?

They are yours. I never put a child’s face online, and nothing at all without your written yes. With your permission I may show a drawing or a card, without names.

And if it rains?

Every outdoor day has an indoor twin. We choose together the evening before.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free until 48 hours before. Within 48 hours I ask half, because I kept the day for you. If your child is ill, nothing.

Do you take babies?

Sometimes, one at a time, once we have met. The days above are written for ages three to twelve.

Who are you?

Wietske Hoencamp. I live in Amsterdam. I coach, photograph, teach skiing and study psychology. Wonder Nanny grew out of L’arte dell’amore, the small house where the rest of my work lives.
